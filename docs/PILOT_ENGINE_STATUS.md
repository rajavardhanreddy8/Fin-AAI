# Shared pilot engine milestone

Updated 2026-10-07. This is a deterministic simulation engine, not a live broker executor or a qualified trading policy.

## Frozen execution behavior

- Input is NIFTYBEES completed five-minute **bar-start** OHLCV. Naive timestamps mean IST; aware timestamps normalize to IST. Nonfinite values, malformed candles, negative volume, duplicates, unsorted inputs and nonregular timestamps are rejected. Replay requires 35 records; the runner may use `_validated_bars(..., minimum=1)` at its input boundary.
- A rules-only replay is `replay(bars)` with no QPolicy. Entry requires the completed close to exceed the previous 20 highs and the current session VWAP. ATR14 uses true range, including the previous close and current completed bar, initialized by the first 14 ranges then Wilder smoothing.
- Initial capital is INR5,000, one long position, no leverage or top-ups. Whole-unit sizing limits planned stop loss **including declared entry/exit fees and adverse stop slippage** to 0.25% of preentry equity and limits purchase plus fees to available cash. Unfilled entries remain unfilled. A partial fill is the resulting whole-unit quantity, with no fabricated remainder fill.
- Initial stop is two ATR below the actual slipped entry fill; target is twice that initial stop distance above entry. Stops and targets are then fixed. Opening stop/target gaps are resolved before later intrabar touches; stop wins if both levels touch after the open and their order is unknown. A gap below stop exits at the adverse opening price; favorable gaps above target receive the target price. Protective fills are exempt from the liquidity entry cap.
- New entries are blocked once session loss reaches 1%, once an incomplete session/gap is observed, or at 15:00 IST. Session liquidation begins at the observed 15:15 open. A missing liquidation bar cannot be invented; an overnight position exits at the next observed open and records an execution defect.
- Equity drawdown is monitored at observed opens and closes. Reaching 5% latches `paused`, liquidates at the next observed executable open, and prevents further entries across session/checkpoint resets. No automated reactivation exists. Intrabar peaks cannot be established reliably from OHLC; this sampling convention is an assumption requiring prospective validation.
- A completed close decision becomes available at bar start plus five minutes plus declared latency. No fill occurs before that time. Delayed orders wait for an eligible observed open. Gaps cancel pending entries and liquidate held positions conservatively; the remainder of that affected session cannot open a position.
- End of history cancels pending decisions and records any open position as unresolved. It never fabricates a terminal liquidation. Each session records its first/last observed bar, bar count and regular-session completeness; an incomplete terminal session records a defect. `terminal=False` keeps an in-progress session incomplete without treating that prefix as a final defect and retains the open position and last pending decision for full-prefix forward-paper rebuilds.

## Declared assumptions, not verified tariffs

`ExecutionAssumptions` defaults are **10 basis points per-side fees, minimum INR1 per fill, 5 basis points adverse slippage per side, one second decision latency, and 1% participation in the previous completed candle's volume**. These are sensitivity inputs. The owner's ICICI plan, ETF applicability, taxes, actual spreads, liquidity, partial fills, protection fills and API latency are unverified. Fees and slippage are included in net equity/rewards and separately reported as simulation costs; compute/model expenses require the separate provider ledger.

No spread or actual fill is inferred from five-minute OHLCV. Market-order availability at observed opens, protective-order availability inside a candle, full liquidation fills and sampled equity drawdown are declared simulation conventions. A provider may set a more conservative latency assumption; a str-returning callback alone cannot prove its actual response was available by that assumed time.

## Provider and persistence interface

```python
result = replay(
    bars, policy=None,
    decide=None,  # decide(evidence: dict, rl_action: str | None) -> str
    assumptions=ExecutionAssumptions(),
    initial_state=None, training=False, seed=42, terminal=True,
    entry_start_at=None,  # optional observed bar for a fresh evaluation window
)
record_run(db_path, run_id, "backtest", None, result)
```

The callback receives `instrument`, `bar`, `decision_at`, `breakout`, `above_vwap`, `atr`, `state`, `position`, `cash_inr`, `equity_inr`, `rules_action` and `constraints`. Valid outputs are BUY/HOLD/SELL/ABSTAIN. Models may veto a permitted entry or request an early exit. They cannot invent an entry or alter stops, targets, sizing, cutoffs or loss pauses. Exceptions and invalid outputs produce ABSTAIN and record a defect; protective exits continue independently.

`entry_start_at` must name an observed 09:15 bar and cannot be combined with a checkpoint. Earlier bars warm breakout/ATR/VWAP calculations, but produce no trades, RL actions or model calls; cash and risk state start fresh for that window. The run summary records the selected start. An ordinary full-history replay continues to carry drawdown pauses through later sessions.

`ReplayResult` preserves the existing list of seven-field transition tuples and adds `trades`, `sessions`, `assumptions`, `costs_inr`, `max_drawdown`, `defects`, `terminal`, `variant`, `provenance`, and `final_state`. Variants are rules-only, Jev-only, RL-only and RL+Jev. `record_run` supports `policy=None` and optional `policy_version`; default policy versions are frozen-rules-v1 or the supplied QPolicy.version.

SQLite retains old tables and adds completed trades, observed sessions and per-run checkpoints. Migration adds terminal and engine-version columns plus session completeness fields. Old sessions default to incomplete because their full observation history was not persisted. Old transitions retain version1 and are excluded from training because their reward and position attribution cannot be repaired from stored evidence alone. Historical `mode="paper"` records explicitly retain `historical-replay` provenance and cannot count as forward paper evidence.

Checkpoints preserve cash, position, fixed protection prices, high-water mark and latched session/drawdown state. They also mark any unresolved decision, including HOLD/ABSTAIN without a pending order. `initial_state` rejects open decisions and older checkpoints without decision evidence when a prior timestamp exists: rebuild the full prefix using cached decisions instead. This avoids silently losing reward attribution. No new decision is opened at the 15:25 final bar. A session resumed mid-day from a checkpoint stays incomplete for qualification because its opening equity and earlier trades cannot be reconstructed from that checkpoint alone. Checkpoints are simulation state, not broker reconciliation. Complete observed history and cached provider decisions remain the source for forward restart reconstruction.

## Learning and qualification

Rewards are actual marked-to-market equity change after fees and slipped fills, including HOLD and no-trade intervals. A delayed action accumulates its observed interval reward until execution or cancellation. The following state records the resulting position and unrealized sign. Forced exits, failed fills, invalid rule requests and model overrides never receive a matched RL label. Terminal transitions use zero future value; next-state action values are restricted to actions valid for the resulting position.

`replay(..., training=True, seed=42)` uses 0.1 exploration. Deployed replay uses zero exploration. The CLI-compatible `train_candidate` makes ten chronological offline passes through verified matched transitions with alpha0.1 and gamma0.99; it cannot explore new actions from transition-only evidence. `train_candidate_from_bars(db_path, audited_csv, version, seed=42)` verifies the dataset manifest, splits complete sessions 60/20/20, replays only the first 60% with seeded exploration for ten episodes, and updates from unchanged executed RL actions. It stores the dataset hash, split dates, assumptions, seed and matched-transition count with the candidate. Validation and holdout bars are not used for training. This path is RL-only; RL+Jev training still requires exact cached provider decisions and verified model/cost evidence. Candidate version names cannot overwrite existing or active policies and there is no automatic activation.

`compare_holdout(db_path, audited_csv, candidate_version, jev_actions=..., rl_jev_actions=...)` reloads the frozen candidate and refuses a changed dataset, split or execution assumptions. It runs rules-only and RL-only with the same fresh holdout risk state; optional model variants require a complete map keyed by the SHA-256 of the exact canonical `{evidence, rl_action}` packet. A missing or invalid action aborts the comparison rather than becoming an ABSTAIN result. Every report is stored under its own content hash in `pilot_comparisons`; `pilot-status` exposes its hash integrity and qualification also verifies its candidate version, dataset hash, split and assumptions against the persisted candidate. The four comparison variants each require 100 untouched completed holdout trades. The report names supplied maps `caller-supplied-unverified`: packet hashes establish reproducibility, not a Jev invocation, resolved model identity or provider cost. A partial two-variant report explicitly lists the missing decision sets; all reports retain `qualified=false` until external evidence is verified.

`audit_forward_paper(path)` reads the paper journal without changing it. It excludes injected or legacy clocks, checks bar observation time, one canonical official-calendar payload that admits every observed bar, exact decision packet hashes, decision latency, full 75-bar sessions and trade hashes, and rebuilds completed trades through the shared engine. `qualification(db, forward_db_path=...)` uses this audit rather than any `pilot_runs` rows labelled paper or manually relabelled as forward. The audit proves consistency of the saved simulation journal, not independent market-source authenticity or real broker fills; those and verified costs/model evidence remain open.

Qualification is deliberately unavailable in this milestone. It still requires 100 untouched holdout completed trades for each compared variant, 30 genuine forward paper completed trades across 30 sessions, positive expectancy after verified trading costs, drawdown below5%, no unresolved execution defects, RL+Jev improvement over Jev-only under identical assumptions and stricter live release gates. Old `status="qualified"` rows and manual activation flags cannot bypass missing evidence. No broker calls, activation endpoint, credential reads or infrastructure deployment were added.
