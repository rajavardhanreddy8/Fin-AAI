# 30-day finance, markets, agents and ML course

This course uses the fin-AAI workspace as the lab. Each session takes about 30 minutes: 10 minutes of concepts, 15 minutes in the project, and 5 minutes writing one evidence-backed conclusion. The course teaches decision quality; it does not promise profit.

## Week 1 — Market and evidence foundations

| Day | Topic | Project exercise | Evidence to save |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Equity ownership, exchanges and orders | Open Momentum Finance and identify investing, trading and paper records | A note separating a company fact from a market price |
| 2 | NSE symbols, ISINs and series | Inspect the current 500-company discovery seed | One identity and one source URL |
| 3 | Price, volume, liquidity and affordability | Compare share price with market capitalisation and tradability | A short affordability-vs-value note |
| 4 | OHLCV and corporate actions | Read the paper-bar validation rules | One example of a rejected bar |
| 5 | Returns, deposits and compounding | Reconcile ₹10,000 initial capital and ₹2,000 monthly contribution | Contribution-adjusted return calculation |
| 6 | Fees, slippage and gaps | Change the trading cost assumptions in the lab | Net vs gross result |
| 7 | Weekly review | Review the first week’s notes and list three unknowns | A dated evidence checklist |

## Capstone standard

A completed memo must include the as-of date, source documents, missing-data labels, deterministic calculations, costs, benchmark, drawdown, sample size, paper status and a clear decision gate. It must distinguish supplied facts from interpretation and must not turn a low share price or a positive backtest into a promise of profit.
